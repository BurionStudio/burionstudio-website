THE STUDIO

Independent by nature.

Burion Studio is an independent software studio building games, applications and digital products from idea to release.

What Burion Studio does

Burion Studio focuses on turning ideas into focused digital products. The studio works across interactive games, practical applications and digital experiences, with an emphasis on clear design, useful systems and long-term usability.

Projects are developed independently, allowing product direction, design and implementation to evolve together rather than around a fixed template.

A small studio with a long-term view
