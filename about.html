About Burion Studio

Independent software studio · Founded by İrfan Aslan BÜRİAN

Who we are

Burion Studio is an independent software studio founded by İrfan Aslan BÜRİAN. It is being built around a simple idea: turn thoughtful concepts into useful, well-designed digital products. The studio works independently, with a focus on clear user experiences, reliable systems, and products that can grow over time.

What we build

The studio's areas of interest include games, applications, websites, and other digital products. Work begins with understanding the problem a product should solve, then shaping its experience and functionality through design, development, and iteration. The goal is not to add complexity for its own sake, but to make each product understandable, useful, and enjoyable to use.

Current projects

Projects shown on this website reflect their stated status. Trivia is a competitive quiz game in development, designed around knowledge, speed, and progression. An agricultural platform is at the concept stage and explores ways digital tools could support smarter decisions in farming. These descriptions communicate current direction, not a guarantee of launch dates, features, or availability. Project details may change as research and development continue.

How we work

Burion Studio is currently an independently operated, founder-led studio. That means projects are developed with direct attention to product decisions, implementation, usability, and long-term maintenance. The studio aims to communicate clearly about what exists today and what remains a concept, and to build its portfolio gradually rather than overstate its size or capabilities.
