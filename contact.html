Contact Burion Studio

Public enquiries · Email contact

General enquiries

For questions about Burion Studio, its website, current projects, or potential collaborations, please email support@burionstudio.com. This is the studio's public contact address and the only contact channel currently published on this website.

Selecting the email link opens the email application configured on your device. Include a clear subject and enough context to help explain your question or proposal. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information in an initial enquiry.

Projects and collaboration

Burion Studio develops independent games, applications, and digital products. Enquiries related to project information, feedback, technical questions about the website, or possible collaboration are welcome. Please describe the relevant project or idea and what kind of response you are looking for. Any potential collaboration or project commitment would need to be discussed and confirmed directly by email.

Response expectations

Messages are reviewed as time and project work allow. A response is not guaranteed within a particular timeframe, and sending an enquiry does not create a business, employment, support, or contractual relationship. If your message requires a reply, include a valid reply-to address in your email client.

Business details

Burion Studio is an independently operated software studio. A public telephone number and postal business address are not currently provided. Email is the appropriate way to initiate contact; please do not assume that the studio offers phone support, walk-in visits, or a physical customer service location.

Privacy

Information you send by email is handled through the email services used by you and the recipient. For details about website activity and email correspondence, please review the Privacy Policy. To learn more about the studio, visit the About page.
